      How you became a tester

      1. Sign up once on FirstLook. That puts you in the pool
      2. The studio picks from the pool and emails invites in waves, sometimes after a hardware or attendance survey
      3. Picked players get the tester role, switched on by the studio (Discord post, Jun 23)

      What the points did

      • Quests unlock the first ranks: 180 points once, then 10 a month
      • WARDOG at 1,000 is out of reach by quests: about 7 years. The studio hands out WARDOG and up
      • The studio called them cosmetic: "strictly Discord Server-related cosmetic rewards" (Steam reply, Feb 6)
      • Why run them: they keep people wishlisting and posting. The sign-up page says this "maximises your chances", with no weight given

      Quest points against the 1,000 WARDOG needs

      Who does what

      The player
      • Signs up on a WARDOGS-branded FirstLook page
      • Wishlists the game on Steam, chats in the WARDOGS Discord
      • Claims a Discord rank when the points allow
      FirstLook, the service
      • Runs the sign-up page, the points dashboard and a Discord bot
      • Reads Steam to confirm wishlists, counts Discord messages
      • Handed out Steam playtest keys until Steam capped them in August; still hands out Discord roles
      BULKHEAD, the studio
      • Chooses the quests, point values and rank names; hands out the top three ranks by hand
      • Posts the instructions in a Discord channel
      • Gets verified wishlists, an active server and one identity per player

      What a player actually does five steps, screenshot beside each. Click a screenshot to enlarge

      1Open the sign-up page
      • Where: community.wardogs.com, a page FirstLook hosts for the studio
      • What you see: the trailer, a paragraph, two buttons: "Sign up with Discord" or "Sign up with Steam"
      • What it says: slots are limited, this is a real test, wishlist and join the Discord to improve your chances
      Web page
      2Link Steam and Discord
      • What happens: a FirstLook account is created and both logins are attached to it
      • Discord: FirstLook's bot adds you to the WARDOGS server
      • Steam: you set "Game details" to Public in your profile privacy settings, so FirstLook can see your wishlist. You may set it back afterwards
      Steam settings
      3Do the tasks on Steam and Discord
      • A quest is a task FirstLook can check automatically. Nothing happens on the FirstLook site, and nothing happens in the game.
      • On Steam: add WARDOGS to your wishlist for 100 points. FirstLook reads the wishlist through Steam's public data.
      • In the WARDOGS Discord server: introduce yourself (30 points), send your first five messages (50), then post once a month (10). FirstLook's bot counts them.
      Steam and Discord
      4Watch the points on the dashboard
      • Where: the Rewards tab of your FirstLook dashboard, a web page with tabs Dashboard, Discord, Rewards, Account
      • What you see: a points bar ("0 points, 100 until the next tier"), a "Refresh Steam Wishlist Data" button, the quest list
      • Known problem: points arrive late. BULKHEAD on Steam, Feb 6: "there is a known delay, the team is working on it"
      FirstLook dashboard
      5Claim a rank
      • What a rank is: a Discord role, a tag shown next to your name in the WARDOGS server (@WARPUP, @WARHOUND and so on)
      • How: cross a point threshold, press "Claim Rewards" on the Rewards tab. The four quests reach WARHOUND at most; WARDOG and above come from the studio
      • What it is worth: status. BULKHEAD calls the ranks "strictly Discord Server-related cosmetic rewards". The Steam test key came through sign-up, not through ranks

      What to learn

      • Each step bigger than the last: 100, 1.3k, 5.5k, 10k, 25k, then ~500k in Beta 1 (2 to 20 times each step)
      • Use it or lose it: unclaimed invites were taken back within a day, and missed attendance emails cost the spot
      • Small tests had a job: 4 people for a hardware check, 1,000 for a load test, six servers for a backend test
      • Only about 15% played at once in June, so invite 6 to 7 times the players you need online

      Playbook for our gameThe WARDOGS sequence as a checklist we can adapt

      Our launch checklist, phase by phase

      The WARDOGS moves that worked, put in order, with the timing they used. Shift the timings to our own launch date. proposal

      1. M-7Reveal day
        • Trailer, Steam page, sign-up page and Discord live within the same hour
        • Publisher press release the same day
        • Milestone post at the first round number
      2. M-7 to M-4Build the base
        • One Q&A or devlog video a month
        • Gameplay Shorts every week
        • Giveaways whose entry is sign up, wishlist and repost
        • Volunteer moderators before 25k members
      3. M-5 to M-2Small tests
        • Survey, then invite waves that grow each time
        • NDA: impressions allowed, no footage
        • Confirm attendance within 24 hours or lose the seat
      4. M-3Showcase
        • One big showcase trailer
        • Ask the community to push the store page and show chat
        • Post the full gameplay on YouTube the same hour
      5. D-35 to D-27Alpha and pre-orders
        • A short NDA test, embargo lifted right after
        • Pre-order = guaranteed seat in the next beta
        • Launch pre-orders with a self-mocking video
      6. D-20 to D-4Open betas
        • Lift the NDA and invite streamers to the biggest beta
        • Stress-test the login queue in a one-evening test
        • Drops for watching streams; open to all for the last days
      7. D-dayLaunch
        • Status post every 30 to 60 minutes while servers struggle
        • Nobody skips the queue, creators included
        • Behind-the-scenes recap video the next day
      8. D+1 onAfter launch
        • Weekly player-clip highlights
        • Announce the next season within two weeks
        • Never answer complaints with "don't buy it"
